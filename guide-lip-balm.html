How can I make natural lip balm at home?

You melt waxes, butters and oils together, add essential oils at the right temperature and pour into tubes. My free guide has one tested formula with 7 ingredients. It takes about 20 minutes and makes 20 tubes.

Can I make lip balm without beeswax?

Yes. My free guide includes a vegan version among its customization options, so you can make it without beeswax.

Is lip balm hard to make for a beginner?
