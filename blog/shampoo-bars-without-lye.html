Can you make shampoo bars without lye?

Yes. A syndet shampoo bar uses gentle cleansers instead of lye, so it isn't soap. What a syndet bar is, how it differs from soap and how it's made.

By Yana in Phuket · November 6, 2026

The short answer

Yes. A shampoo bar made with gentle cleansers instead of lye is called a syndet bar, short for synthetic detergent. It isn't soap at all. You melt and mix the ingredients, press them into a mold and never handle lye. A syndet bar works much like a liquid shampoo in solid form.

Soap bars and shampoo bars are different chemistry

Soap is made by saponification, a reaction between oils and lye (sodium hydroxide). The result is alkaline, usually around pH 9 to 10. That's fine for washing hands, but hair and scalp prefer a slightly acidic environment. Washing hair with soap can leave it feeling rough or coated, especially in hard water, where soap leaves a film behind.

A syndet bar uses mild cleansers, called surfactants, that clean at a skin-friendly pH. They're the same kind of cleansers you'd find in a gentle liquid shampoo, just in solid form.

What does syndet mean?

Syndet is short for synthetic detergent. Synthetic can sound scary, but many of these cleansers start from coconut or other plant oils and are then processed in a lab. That's also why I'm open about my own shampoo bar. It uses gentle lab-made cleansers, because that's what makes it work.

How is a syndet bar made?

Without giving away a formula, the process is simple.

  1. Weigh the cleansers, conditioning ingredients and butters or oils.
  2. Melt them gently together into a thick paste.
  3. Let it cool a little, then add the heat-sensitive ingredients, like fragrance, botanicals and preservative.
  4. Press the mixture firmly into molds and let the bars harden.

There's no lye and no weeks of curing like cold process soap. My bar takes about an hour of hands-on time.

Safety notes

Some cleansers come as fine powders that you shouldn't breathe in. Wear a mask and gloves, and work in a well-ventilated room. And like any bar that meets water every day, a shampoo bar needs a preservative to stay fresh between washes.

Are shampoo bars good for your hair?

A well-made shampoo bar cleans like a liquid shampoo, just without the water and the plastic bottle. What matters is mild cleansers that wash without stripping. Hair is personal, so start with a small batch and see how your hair feels after a couple of weeks.

Make your shampoo bar last longer

  • Let it dry between uses on a draining soap dish.
  • Keep it out of the shower stream.
  • Cut a big bar in half and use one piece at a time.
  • Rub it between your hands or straight onto wet hair, whichever you prefer.
