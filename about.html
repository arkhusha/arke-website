ARK Experience is a personal project — one maker, one kitchen in Phuket, and formulas refined until they're worth sharing.

The maker

Hi, I'm Yana.

By day, I work in marketing — eight hours at a screen, every day. A few years ago I started craving the opposite: something slow, tactile, and real. Something I could hold in my hands at the end of it.

That craving took me from polymer clay to candles to my first soap workshop in Bangkok — and then all the way into natural body care. I've studied and made it in Thailand, Malaysia, Canada, and online, and the formulas I share are the ones I use every single day.

ARK Experience is my way of passing that on: not products to buy, but knowledge to keep — explained the way I wish someone had explained it to me when I started.

How it started

From curiosity to craft.

2022

The craving for something real

Tired of screens, I went looking for a hands-on creative outlet. It started with polymer clay, then candles — anything I could shape with my own hands.

The turning point

A soap workshop in Bangkok

My first soap-making workshop at Bangkok Soap Opera changed everything. Suddenly body care wasn't a product on a shelf — it was a craft I could learn.

The deep dive

Learning, batch by batch

I studied natural body care in Thailand, Malaysia, Canada, and online — testing, failing, reformulating, and slowly building formulas that hold up in Phuket's year-round heat.

Today

ARK Experience

The formulas I make are used daily — by me, my family, and my friends. Now I'm turning them into guides, so you can understand and make them too.

What I stand by

Four quiet promises.

Educate, don't preach

Plain-language science, never fear. You'll learn what each ingredient does — not what to be scared of.

Tested, then shared

Nothing reaches a guide until it's been made, used, and reformulated in my own kitchen — often for years.

Honest about nature

Natural means origin, not automatic safety. What matters is formulation and concentration — that's what I teach.

Beginner-safe, always

Every guide is written for your very first batch. You don't need craft skills — you need a thermometer and a scale.
