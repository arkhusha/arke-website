What can I use instead of baking soda in deodorant?

Most baking-soda-free deodorants rely on ingredients that absorb moisture, such as clay, together with ingredients that keep odor in check. That's how my formula works. The guide explains every ingredient, why it's there and what changes if you swap it.

Does natural deodorant work in hot, humid weather?

It can, when the formula is made for heat. I live in Phuket and use this deodorant every day in tropical heat. It controls odor rather than stopping sweat, because it's a deodorant, not an antiperspirant. The guide also shows how to keep the stick firm when it's hot.

Is homemade deodorant gentle on sensitive skin?

Skin is personal, so I always suggest a patch test before you use anything new. I made this formula without baking soda because baking soda doesn't agree with every underarm. If your skin is sensitive or you have a skin condition, please check with a doctor first.

What do I need to make it?

A digital scale, a thermometer, a hand mixer, twist-up tubes and a respirator mask for fine powders. The guide lists every item and walks you through melting, mixing and pouring, step by step.
